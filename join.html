Join the coalition uniting South Carolina's horse industry — from breeders to fans of the Carolina Cup.

The South Carolina Equine Alliance is a coalition of groups and individuals dedicated to promoting and growing the horse industry here in our state. SCEA members represent every segment of our industry: from thoroughbred breeders to harness racers to the farmers who grow our feed to fans of the Carolina Cup. We unite interests across South Carolina to advocate for the horse industry and help it grow in the years ahead.
